House rules

Break from behind the head string. A break that pots nothing and drives fewer than four balls to a cushion is a foul.
• Open table until someone legally pots a ball after the break — whichever group that ball belongs to becomes theirs.
• Fouls — hitting the wrong group first, hitting nothing, potting the cue ball, or failing to reach a cushion after contact. Any foul gives your opponent the cue ball in hand, anywhere on the table.
• Pot one of yours and you shoot again. Clear your group, then call a pocket and sink the 8 there to win. The 8 down early, in the wrong pocket, or with a scratch loses the frame.

How the frame is safe & fair

The contract is a pure escrow + shot log — the rules run in your browser, and every shot lands on-chain in order.
• The physics has no floats in it. Positions, speeds, angles and square roots are exact integers, so two browsers replaying the same log always agree ball for ball — there is no rounding for either side to exploit.
• The rack is drawn by the chain. Joining pins a block height that hasn't been mined yet, and its hash shuffles the triangle — neither player nor the node can fish for a break.
• Stall or dispute: after the shot clock either player claims a refund. Concede / agree / refund are the only outcomes — no one can ever be robbed. The contract is upgradable.
