🎮 All apps

Peer-to-peer collateralised loans on the NADO execution layer. A lender posts terms — how much, what interest, what collateral, how long — and escrows the money up front. A borrower posts the collateral and walks away with the principal. Repay before the deadline and the collateral comes back; miss it and the lender takes it. No price oracle, no variable rate, no liquidation bots — interest is a fixed amount and the only trigger is a deadline the chain can see by itself.

Wallet
