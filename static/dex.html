Pools

Every pool is a NADO ↔ asset pair. Tap one to trade it or add liquidity.

Liquidity

A call carries exactly one asset, so adding liquidity is two deposits then a join, staged into a position you own. Anything you have staged but not joined is always refundable — pick a position id and keep using it.

The first provider sets the opening price. After that the NADO side sets how many shares you mint, and the token side must cover its proportional part — any excess stays staged.

Open a pool

Pair any execution-layer asset with NADO. Opening is permissionless and free — it just claims an id; the pool is empty until someone provides liquidity.

The pool contract is public and verifiable on the NADO execution layer. Providing liquidity carries impermanent loss — if the price moves, withdrawing returns a different mix than you put in. Nothing here is investment advice.
