How this works

Each side is its own transaction because a transaction carries one currency. Anything deposited but not yet confirmed is always refundable. The first person to supply a market sets its opening price; after that both sides go in at the market's current ratio, and anything left over stays yours.

Swap NADO against real BTC, ETH or SOL — no custodian, no wrapped coins. New: check every figure and start small.

How an atomic swap works

Both sides lock behind the same secret. The NADO side sits in an L1 HTLC under exactly the same SHA-256 hashlock as the other chain's lock, so one revealed secret opens both and neither party can take one leg without giving up the other. Each row verifies the counterparty's lock for you before you commit yours, and tells you the next step. This book only coordinates — it never holds your coins.

Post an order

Posting costs nothing and locks nothing — funds move only after someone fills.

How this works

Posting creates your swap secret (kept in this browser — back it up). Once filled, your row walks you through the locks. An unfinished swap refunds to whoever paid in once it expires — nothing can get stuck. A Tip on the row pays watchtowers to finish the swap for you.
