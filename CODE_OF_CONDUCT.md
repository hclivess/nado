# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in the NADO project — in the repository, issue tracker, Discord, and
any other project space — is expected to help keep it a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the network's users.
- Assume good faith; disagree on the technical merits.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Shilling, price talk, pump schemes, or soliciting investment in project spaces.
- Social-engineering users for keys, seed phrases, or funds. Maintainers will **never** ask
  for your private key.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers privately on Discord or via the contact in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions and comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban any participant for
behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
